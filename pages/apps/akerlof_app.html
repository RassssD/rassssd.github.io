Akerlof Adverse Selection Model

Workers with types t ∈ [0,1] have outside option r(t). Equilibrium: w* = g(w*) = E[t | r(t) ≤ w*]. Both r(t) and g(w) plotted in the (t,w) plane — intersections with 45° line give efficient cutoff (◆) and CE wages (● stable, ○ unstable). Shaded regions show efficient employment set {t : t ≥ r(t)}.
